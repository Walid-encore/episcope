# Demand-conversion assumptions (AN-6)

Generated by `analytics/optimise/demand.py`. **These values are placeholders chosen for this prototype. They are not taken from MoH or WHO protocols; replace them with sourced values or present them as stated assumptions.** `assumption_sensitivity()` shows how much each one moves the totals.

| Quantity | Dengue | Diarrhoeal | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| los_days | 4.0 | 2.0 | Mean length of stay (days) |
| saline_units_per_admission | 4.0 | 3.0 | IV saline units per admitted case |
| ns1_tests_per_case | 0.5 | 0.0 | Share of cases given an NS1 test |
| ors_sachets_per_case | 0.0 | 6.0 | ORS sachets per case |
| fallback_admission_rate | 0.15 | 0.05 | Admission rate used if a district has no history |

- NS1 wastage: 10% extra kits.
- Admission rate: observed admitted/cases per district and disease, shrunk toward the national rate with k = 50 pseudo-cases.

## Formulae

- admissions = cases x admission_rate
- beds = admissions x length_of_stay / 7 (Little's law, steady state)
- IV saline = admissions x saline units per admission
- NS1 kits = cases x tests per case x (1 + wastage)
- ORS sachets = cases x sachets per case

## Uncertainty

- `*_q90` columns apply the same formulae to the forecast's 90th percentile (forecast uncertainty only).
- Beds are peak weekly occupancy over the horizon; consumables are summed over weeks and diseases.
- Not modelled: carry-over of patients admitted before the forecast window, admission rates rising during outbreaks, and district-specific clinical practice.
